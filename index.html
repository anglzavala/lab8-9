the original idea

A portfolio that does more than show.

Project Orbit turns a collection of student assignments into a guided, interactive studio. Visitors can explore work, ask questions, see sample data, browse a mock shop, and use an assistant to operate supported parts of the site.

Problem: Student work often lives across disconnected tools and is difficult to explore. Orbit’s objective is to bring those pieces together in one consistent, accessible application.

Who benefits? Students presenting their progress, educators reviewing it, and visitors who want a clearer way to understand the projects.

how the agents work

Understand → select → execute → confirm

The assistant routes a request to one of the site’s bounded action handlers. Each handler updates a real interface element; the reply confirms the result.

security, privacy & documentation

Clear controls. No surprise checkout.

Voice recognition requires browser support and your permission; use the visible microphone control to start or stop it. Speech recognition may be processed by your browser vendor, so check that service’s privacy terms. Text commands are handled locally in this page. The sample cart is not connected to a seller or payment processor. Only use media you own or are licensed to share.

Known limitations: This prototype’s assistant recognizes a predefined set of commands, rather than understanding every possible phrasing. Browsers may require a click before audio/video playback. Voice and media features should be tested in your target browser. Assignment content shown here is a demo and should be replaced with your actual course submissions.
